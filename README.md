# Bison-Connect
Site - https://p3nda-f.github.io/Bison-Connect/
Demo - https://youtu.be/ViJUUrhWD0Y

Bison Mentor Match links Howard University students with alumni mentors through an interactive platform. Users take a profile quiz and use a Tinder-like swipe feature for compatibility. Mutual matches enable direct communication, fostering professional relationships that enhance career development within the Bison community.

Bison Mentor Match: Connecting the Mecca's Past, Present, and Future: 
As one of the nation's premier Historically Black Colleges and Universities (HBCUs) and a top-ranked private research institution, Howard University ("The Mecca") has long served as a powerhouse for academic excellence, leadership, and cultural impact. For generations, its campus has produced an extraordinary roster of accomplished alumni who have shaped global industries—from trailblazing political leaders like Kamala Harris and Supreme Court Justice Thurgood Marshall, to cultural icons and artists like Chadwick Boseman, Taraji P. Henson, and Toni Morrison, as well as business visionaries like media mogul Cathy Hughes and capital management founder Eddie C. Brown. The depth, influence, and success of the Bison network are unmatched. Countless current students stand to benefit immeasurably from tapping into this reservoir of wisdom, career advice, and sponsorship.

A Modern Solution for a Timeless Vision: 
For years, the Howard University Student Government Association (HUSA) and various campus groups have worked tirelessly to bridge the gap by manually connecting mentors with students. However, those efforts often faced administrative bottlenecks or lacked scale.
Bison Mentor Match solves this by providing a sleek, centralized, and accessible digital platform built for every student and alumnus. Rooted in real-world community conversations—such as interactions with visiting alumni entrepreneurs and small business owners who want to actively give back and support the next generation of Bison—this platform turns inspiration into direct, actionable mentorship.

How It Works: The Interactive Experience: 
The application leverages a modern, engaging interface to make finding the right professional connection seamless:

The Onboarding Quiz: Users select their role (Alumni or Student) and step through a streamlined 4-part profile questionnaire. They input their contact details, academic majors, graduation years, career goals or current professions, geographic locations, hobbies, and specific mentorship focus areas (such as tech entrepreneurship, wealth management, civil rights, or medicine).

The Tinder-Style Swipe Deck: Instead of sorting through dry, static spreadsheets or rigid directory lists, users enter an interactive matching deck. The system computes dynamic compatibility scores and highlights key shared traits—such as matching majors, overlapping hometowns, or mutual hobby interests. Users can review rich profile cards to learn why they match, then tap a heart to express interest or pass with an X.

Mutual-Match Reveal & Chat: When both parties express mutual interest, a celebratory match modal triggers immediately, securely unlocking contact info like email and phone numbers. From there, users transition straight into an integrated, real-time messaging system to coordinate schedules, ask for guidance, and build lasting professional relationships.
